# The Machine-Legible Business

*This page moved. It is now [The Machine-Drivable Business](/concepts/the-machine-drivable-business).*

---

The rename happened on 2026-07-28 for one reason, and the reason is on the new page in full.

Legible means readable, and reading is only one of the two properties the definition always required. The other is that a machine can work the controls. A business that can be perfectly read and not acted on is an excellent dashboard, and a dashboard leaves the owner as the integration layer, which is the exact position the concept exists to get them out of.

Driving is both. You read the road and you turn the wheel.

This stub stays so links to the old address keep resolving. Everything worth reading is at [The Machine-Drivable Business](/concepts/the-machine-drivable-business).
