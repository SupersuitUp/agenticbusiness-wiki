# The Boutique Corporate Attorney

*Corporate lawyer running a small general-corporate practice organized around matters, working out what changes when a firm's judgment and its work product both become version-controlled assets. Source of the principle on version-controlling the work product.*

---

## Why this is an archetype

This wiki anonymizes operators who shared their principles in private conversation with the wiki's stewards. Each one is published under an archetype name that captures the substance of their operating posture without identifying them. The conversations are real, the dates are recorded, and the wisdom is theirs. The names are not on the wiki.

The Boutique Corporate Attorney is the archetype of a solo or near-solo professional whose entire margin comes from judgment, whose work product is text, and who is early in deciding what to do about that. The principles attributed to this archetype come from one specific practitioner working through exactly that question.

## Background

He runs a small corporate practice doing general corporate work: agreements, markups, entity questions, the ordinary load of a business's outside counsel. Work is organized into matters, and within a matter into tasks. One associate, hired recently, with onboarding currently handled by an email.

He came to the conversation without an agentic system and without a document corpus, which is what makes his contribution useful. The wiki's other mentors mostly describe systems they already run. He is the operator at the moment of decision, and the questions he asked are the ones every professional in his position will ask.

## Why he is on this wiki

Professional services is the least examined and most exposed category in the agentic transition. The work is abstract, text-driven, and priced on judgment, which makes it a near-perfect fit for the pattern this wiki describes and a near-perfect target for the firms that adopt it first.

His specific contribution is the correction he applied to himself. Told that version-controlling his work would let an agent learn from it, he immediately identified the gap: a version history shows what changed and says nothing about why, and an instruction is not an explanation. That observation is now the load-bearing half of the principle.

He also named the asset argument from the employee's side, which is the direction operators rarely think from:

> "If I were to go work somewhere else, I could say: when you guys hire me, you can get access to all this data."
>
> — The Boutique Corporate Attorney, 2026-08-24

And he named why his profession is the sharpest test case:

> "Law is a uniquely good candidate for this sort of thing because it is so abstract. Every time you do something, the words are slightly different."
>
> — The Boutique Corporate Attorney, 2026-08-24

## Principles contributed to this wiki

- **[Version Control the Work Product](/principles/version-control-the-work-product)**: put every document the business produces into a private repository along with the reasoning that produced it, so the firm's past output becomes a corpus its agents can mine.

He is also the practitioner the concept [The Firm Super Suit](/concepts/the-firm-super-suit) was worked out against.
